Our Purpose

A Future Leo
Should Have Had

Leo's Angels was founded in memory of Leo Alan O'Brien, who died from Sudden Unexpected Death in Epilepsy (SUDEP) two weeks after his eleventh birthday.

Leo should still be here.

His life and his family's experience revealed the severe challenges created by fragmented information, disconnected services and the heavy responsibility placed on families to coordinate complex care. Leo's Angels exists to turn that experience into meaningful change.

By bringing together families, clinicians, researchers, healthcare leaders and technology specialists, we are working towards a future where every child living with epilepsy receives safer, more connected and more compassionate care.
